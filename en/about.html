Technical Strength & Team Building

ChuanDao AI has built a core team of "practical + innovative" members, with over 120 current members, of which technical R&D personnel account for more than 70%, and 85% have more than 6 years of software development experience. Core team members all come from major internet companies and leading technology enterprises, having led the development of projects with tens of millions of users, such as financial risk control systems, intelligent manufacturing management platforms, and new retail data middle platforms.
Technical Director Zhang once led the team to complete the development of an AI quality inspection system for a listed automotive company, improving product detection efficiency by 60% and reducing false positive rate to below 0.3%.
We always adhere to the concept of "technology feeding back to business", establishing special R&D funds and investing 20% of annual revenue in AI algorithm optimization, low-code platform iteration, and industry solution innovation.
